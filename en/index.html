For manufacturers and exporters

Want to sell your product in Uruguay?

We are a Uruguayan importer and distributor with our own warehouse in Pando, Canelones. We import our own product line and help other companies enter the Uruguayan market.

WhatsAppSend an email

What we do for foreign suppliers

Import

We buy and import the product through a Uruguayan company with a tax ID (RUT), a customs broker and the full customs process.

Permits

We tell you which registrations the product needs in Uruguay (for example food, cosmetics or electrical goods) before any purchase is closed.

Distribution

Warehouse in Pando and an open channel with wholesale distributors, supermarkets and specialist shops.

Local know-how

Spanish labelling, import costs and what the Uruguayan market expects in each category.

What we need to evaluate your product

  • Product data sheet and photos
  • Minimum order quantity and FOB or ex-works price
  • Countries where you already sell and the certificates you hold
  • If the product comes from Mercosur, whether it has a certificate of origin

Each case is assessed individually. We do not handle prohibited goods, or regulated products without the required permit.
